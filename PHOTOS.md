# Photo checklist

Every grey-green box on the site is a photo slot. To fill one, save a photo with the **exact file name** below
into the folder shown, commit and push. Vercel redeploys in about a minute and the photo appears.

- `.jpg` is expected; `.webp`, `.jpeg` and `.png` also work (same name, different extension).
- File names are lower-case with hyphens. `Agam-Gupta.JPG` will not be found; `agam-gupta.jpg` will.
- Keep each file under about 300 KB (export at the size listed, quality 75-80). Large files slow the site.
- Photos are cropped to fill their box, centred. Keep the subject near the middle.
- Portraits in `people/` are shared: one file shows on the Home, Our Story, People and Chartered Accountants pages wherever that person appears.
- Slots without a photo simply keep the green placeholder, so you can add photos gradually.

## Real estate project photos

Project cards on the Real Estate page come from `assets/data/projects.json`. Each project's photo goes in
`assets/images/real-estate/projects/`, named after the `photo` value for that project in projects.json
(for example `"photo": "manesar-warehouse"` → `assets/images/real-estate/projects/manesar-warehouse.jpg`). Recommended size 1200 × 800 px.
