Asset classes

Private markets

Fifty-plus direct cheques since 2014, concentrated in deep tech and AI, fintech, healthcare, defence and consumer. Our deep tech exposure runs through Seafund, which backs the indigenisation of critical technologies in India.

Our investments in other sectors are made through LP positions in sector-specific funds across consumer, fintech, venture debt and other sectors, backing specialist managers who are best placed to find the deal.

  • Deep tech & AI
  • Fintech & insurtech
  • Healthtech & biotech
  • Defence & aerospace
  • Consumer brands

Public markets

A listed Indian equities book running continuously since the 1990s, held directly and through selected PMS and Category III mandates. Balance sheet quality first, then fundamentals, then the patience to do nothing for long periods.

SG Equity, a dedicated proprietary public markets fund, is in formation.

Real estate

Freehold land along NCR growth corridors, held unlevered, alongside the development and hospitality work of SSA Real Estate. Through SGRE we provide structured debt and equity to residential, commercial, warehousing and hospitality projects.
