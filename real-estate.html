How we build

From land to landmark, in four steps.

  1. 01

    Acquire

  2. 02

    Design & build

    Architecture and construction managed by the family, with the financial controls of a CA practice.

  3. 03

    Own

    We keep the assets we build, held on our own balance sheet without short-term leverage.

  4. 04

    Lease or operate

    Leased to strong tenants and established hotel brands today, and increasingly operated by us.

Land

Every project starts with land we know.

Before we build, we assemble. Over two decades we have pieced together parcels, secured licences and held land through cycles until the right use was ready.

Accumulation

Assembling contiguous parcels from multiple owners into sites large enough for warehousing, hotels and commercial use.

Title & licensing

Clear-title diligence, change of land use and development licences, handled with the rigour of our chartered accountancy roots.

Land bank

Freehold holdings along NCR's growth corridors and agricultural land ahead of urban expansion, held unlevered for the long term.

Highest and best use

Converting land into income-producing assets, or partnering with developers, when the corridor and the timing are right.
