The founding practice · Since 1993

Sanjay Satpal & Associates, Chartered Accountants

A firm of chartered accountants in New Delhi, established in 1993, providing audit, taxation and advisory services. The practice is where the SSA group began.

Three decades of audit, taxation and advisory work, delivered by a team of chartered accountants, cost accountants and specialists.

The firm's partners bring between fifteen and thirty-three years each in practice.

Areas of practice

Services

Six areas of practice for companies, closely held businesses, families and individuals.

Photograph · Audit
01

Audit & assurance

Independent audits that give boards, lenders and regulators confidence in the numbers, carried out to ICAI standards.

  • Statutory audit under the Companies Act
  • Tax audit under the Income Tax Act
  • Internal audit and internal financial controls
  • Bank, stock and concurrent audits
Photograph · Direct tax
02

Direct taxation

Tax planning, compliance and representation for companies, firms, families and individuals, from annual filings to complex assessments.

  • Corporate and personal tax advisory
  • Returns, TDS and withholding compliance
  • Scrutiny assessments and reassessments
  • Appeals before CIT(A) and ITAT
Photograph · GST
03

GST & indirect tax

End-to-end support under GST, from registration and monthly returns to input tax credit reviews, audits and departmental proceedings.

  • Registration, returns and reconciliations
  • Input tax credit and refund claims
  • GST audits and advisory on transactions
  • Notices, assessments and appeals
Photograph · Search & seizure
04

Search, seizure & survey

Assistance through Income Tax search, seizure and survey actions, among the most demanding proceedings in Indian tax, from the first day through to final appeal.

  • Support during search and survey actions
  • Review of seized records and statements
  • Block and search assessments
  • Appellate representation before CIT(A) and ITAT
Photograph · Advisory
05

Financial services & structuring

Financial and tax due diligence, transaction structuring and valuation support for acquisitions, investments, restructurings and family arrangements.

  • Financial and tax due diligence
  • Transaction and holding structures
  • Mergers, demergers and restructuring
  • FEMA and cross-border matters
Photograph · Consultancy

Management consultancy

Practical support that keeps a growing business well run: accounting systems, corporate compliance and business advisory.

  • Accounting systems and MIS
  • Company and LLP secretarial compliance
  • Project and business advisory
  • Outsourced accounting support
